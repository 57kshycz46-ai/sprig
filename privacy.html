Sprig Privacy Policy

Effective 1 October 2026

Sprig is a habit tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Sprig does not collect your data.

Information we collect

None. Sprig has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or SDKs. We do not receive, store or sell any personal information about you.

Information stored on your device

The habits you create, their schedules, reminder times and check-in history are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have those turned on; those backups are controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.

You can delete all of it at any time from Settings › Erase all habits inside the app, or by deleting the app.

Purchases

Sprig Pro subscriptions and purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple provides us with aggregated, anonymous sales reports. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's own privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, Sprig asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and you can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you and do not add you to any mailing list.

Children

Sprig is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a future version of Sprig starts collecting any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
